## Scientific limitations that are not defects but bound the claims

* Assay heterogeneity (ATP concentration, cyclin presence) and one censored-data rule (values reported as ">" were dropped; 31% of raw records) are modelling choices with unquantified selection effects. Aggregation takes the median over curated **records**, which are not demonstrably independent experiments.
* Primary-document splitting is neither temporal validation nor a guarantee that train and test share no source papers (a median of 113 paper-split test molecules still share some document with training).
* Scaffold-split separation is not chemical-series independence; the audit still finds a maximum of one test fingerprint overlapping training in a scaffold split.
* The decoys are property-matched putative inactives, not measured inactives, and some descriptors remain imbalanced (the review quotes standardized differences of about -0.5 for HBD in legacy S and MW in corrected S).
* The five scrambled-surrogate searches vary the search against one fixed label permutation; they do not sample over permutations. The size-only control is a size-only regression, not a size-only GA.
* The corrected-versus-legacy comparison bundles three changes (standardization, closed-shell acceptance, deduplication); components were not isolated.
* The frozen plan was written before this campaign's runs but after the project's earlier exploratory work and with historical results already known, so it is not a plan written before all exploration.
* Tests passed (159) in the review's run on 2026-10-05 (`review-2026-10-05/tests.log`, `verification.json`; earlier in this project my own run on the same day also passed). They were **not rerun in this cleanup**, and passing tests do not establish the scientific questions.
